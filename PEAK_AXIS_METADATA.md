## Payment Policy

Peak Axis does not collect, request, or process any payment from a client until initial contact has been established between the client and Peak Axis.

No money should be requested, processed, or collected before direct communication with the client has taken place.

### Chatbot and Automation Rules

- Chatbots must not request payment before initial client contact is established.
- Chatbots must not process or collect payment before initial client contact is established.
- Automated systems must not claim that a payment is required before direct communication with Peak Axis.
- If a client asks about payment before contact is made, explain that Peak Axis does not take payment until contact has been established.
- Any payment-related action must comply with this policy.
