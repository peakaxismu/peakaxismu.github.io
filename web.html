About the Expedition

An island that looks like it's still being made.

Piton de la Fournaise is one of the most active volcanoes on Earth, and one of the most dramatic landscapes in the Indian Ocean. The trek crosses the Plaine des Sables — a barren, lunar plateau — before dropping into the caldera and climbing to the crater rim, where the ground still steams in places.

Landscape

Lava fields, cinder cones and a plateau that feels more like Mars than an island.

Challenge

Long days, altitude, and terrain that demands your full attention underfoot.

Access

A short flight from Mauritius — no long-haul travel required to reach it.

Group

Small groups only, guided throughout by people who know the mountain.

Is This Expedition For You?

Fitness & Difficulty

We'd rather you know exactly what this trek demands before you commit than find out on day two. Read this section properly.

  • Hiking experiencePrior multi-day or full-day hiking experience strongly recommended. This is not a first hike.
  • Fitness levelComfortable walking 15–18km in a day over uneven volcanic terrain, including sustained ascent and descent.
  • AltitudeSummit sits at 2,632m. Mild altitude effects (shortness of breath, fatigue) are common; acute altitude sickness is uncommon at this elevation but not impossible.
  • TerrainVolcanic rock, loose cinder, exposed plateau with little shade or shelter. Sturdy ankle support required.
  • Age & healthRecommended for ages 16+. Please disclose any heart, lung or mobility conditions before booking.
If you're unsure whether this trek is right for your fitness level, get in touch before booking — we'd rather talk it through than have you find out on the trail.
Day By Day

Itinerary

Day 1

Arrival & the Plaine des Sables

Transfer to Réunion and out to the volcano's edge. We cross the Plaine des Sables on foot to reach Gîte du Volcan, our base for the night — a landscape that shifts from green to grey to red within a few kilometres.

Approx. 3–4 hrs walking · Overnight at Gîte du Volcan
Day 2

Pas de Bellecombe to the crater rim

An early start down into the caldera, across the lava fields past Formica Leo, and up to the rim of Dolomieu crater. Weather and activity levels are checked continuously — the route adjusts if conditions call for it. Return to the gîte by evening.

Approx. 6–7 hrs walking · Summit day
Day 3

Return & transfer

A shorter final walk back across the plateau, then transfer back for onward travel or return to Mauritius.

Approx. 2 hrs walking

Your Safety Comes First

Safety on the Mountain

Fournaise is an active volcano, and we treat that seriously. Access to the crater area is subject to the local observatory's alert level, and our guides check conditions before and during every departure.

  • GuidingEvery group is led by a guide familiar with the terrain and current volcanic activity status.
  • Volcanic activityRoutes are adjusted or expeditions postponed if the alert level restricts crater access. Your safety takes priority over the itinerary.
  • CommunicationGuides carry means of emergency communication for sections without mobile coverage.
  • WeatherThe plateau is exposed with fast-changing weather; contingency plans are in place for wind, rain and low visibility.
